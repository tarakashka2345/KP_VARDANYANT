Sleep paralysis

Sleep paralysis is a temporary inability to move or speak that occurs when you are falling asleep or waking up. During an episode, you may also experience hallucinations, such as seeing figures or feeling a presence in the room. While sleep paralysis can be frightening, it is a harmless condition and usually resolves on its own within a few minutes.

Sleepwalking

Sleepwalking, also known as somnambulism, is a sleep disorder that causes people to walk or perform other activities while they are not fully awake. It typically occurs during deep sleep, and individuals who sleepwalk may not remember the episode afterward. While sleepwalking is more common in children, it can also affect adults and is sometimes associated with other sleep disturbances or health conditions.
